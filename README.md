# MARS-MISSION-PLANNER

## What is our Problem?

This is year 2056 , In our journey from Earth to Mars , Scientists launched a Mars Mission MAWRGV-I (Mars Ancient Water Research and Geography of Volcanoes ). The rover successfully landed on Hellas Basin,Mars. The mission of the rover was to find weather there is ice frost in Hellas Basin and do Geography research of volcanoes in Albamons. As rover came out from it's space craft and started moving towards Hellas Basin's deep terrain , the rover wheels experienced some technical issues due to rockey and loose-dust land. At first , Scientists thought that it was only a normal technical error due to high pressure. But rover get damaged and stopped working. Once again ,Scientists faced a mission failure because of Loose-dust and rockey areas on Mars.

## What is our Solution? 

As Scientists faced a mission failure,Scientists get more focused on finding solution. They organized so many Space events and a group of students proposed a project which can help them to do a good landing on Mars and can face the rover failure problem. 
The students created an app for Rover safety route. It is like a Google maps for rover. And the app will show the most safest route for rover. 

## How Scientists uses the Mission Planner ?

12 January,Year 2063 , Scientists again launched a Mars Mission, MAWRGV-II (Mars Ancient Water Research and Geography of Volcanoes-II). The rover landed on Amazonis Planitia of Mars. Scientists choosed Amazonis Planitia for Landing because it has smooth area as compared to Hellas Basin. And for dust problem,  Scientists used dust protected devices and cleaning systems. Now they used the app map planner. They pointed some safest route for rover and now rover was moving towards Albamons for Volcanic Research. Volcanic can be dangerous, So Scientists used the map for route and determined the danger zone like rocks and dept of volcanoes mountain. The rover did so many Research safely. 
10 NOVEMBER,Year 2063 , rover was now moving towards Outflow Channels for ancient water route Research. Now Scientists might face problem in that rockey areas but by help of the rover map planner , their mission seemed little easy.
25 February, Year 2064, Now rover was in Valles Marineris to Research about Water resources. Scientists again used the app for safety route measures. 
7 July , Year 2064 , Scientists were successful in Researching about Ancient Water Resources and Volcanic Geography.
